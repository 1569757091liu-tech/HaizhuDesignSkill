# Motion Sites Adapter

## Role
Provide motion-language and choreography references.

## Consider when
- hero or page transitions need a deliberate motion concept
- scroll choreography, hover behavior, sequencing, or cinematic interaction is requested

## Extract
- motion archetype
- trigger
- sequence
- duration/easing vocabulary
- spatial relationship
- intensity

## Reject
Motion that is purely decorative, blocks the task, harms performance, or has no reduced-motion fallback.

## Output
A semantic motion prompt, not copied source code.
